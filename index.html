MeshCore · community fork · espnow-ip-bridge

Extend your mesh without new radios

Somebody has to put a repeater where the coverage actually is — a rooftop, a hilltop, a spare bedroom with a window. This fork lets everything else reach it without LoRa hardware of its own — a companion at your desk, a room server in the building next door, even another repeater across town — all over WiFi and ESP‑NOW instead of another LoRa hop, without giving up the airtime discipline the mesh depends on.

Why bother with any of this

Basements block RF, WiFi doesn't care

A repeater in a basement or a steel-frame building may never reach the mesh over LoRa at all — but if it has WiFi, it can still fully participate.

Don't burn LoRa airtime for ten feet

A companion sitting right next to its own repeater doesn't need a real LoRa hop just to say hello. That's airtime and duty-cycle budget better spent reaching further.

Fewer radios, same mesh

A companion or room server with no second LoRa radio at all can still join in fully, at a fraction of the hardware cost.

RF still comes first

None of this replaces LoRa. Where a real RF path exists, it's still preferred — this is about reaching where LoRa can't, or shouldn't have to.
